---
title: About
description: The Deep Stack is a technical education, research, and advisory venture for a software world with abundant code and scarce judgment.
---

The Deep Stack exists to help software engineers and engineering organizations understand the systems beneath modern software: runtimes, garbage collectors, databases, networking, operating systems, cloud infrastructure, performance, reliability, security, and practical correctness.

Its central thesis is simple:

> AI will make code generation cheaper while making deep engineering judgment more valuable.

Teams still need engineers who can reason about system behavior, verify correctness, diagnose performance problems, and make architectural decisions under real production constraints.

The venture begins with technical writing, focused advisory work, production-readiness assessments, and workshops. Over time, it will expand into guides, courses, benchmark research, practical certifications, debugging labs, and The Deep Stack Academy.
