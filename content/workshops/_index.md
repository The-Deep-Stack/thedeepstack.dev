Workshops combine mechanisms, production examples, guided investigation, and exercises. They are designed for application, platform, and infrastructure engineers who want stronger systems intuition.

## Initial workshop areas

- Performance engineering for backend engineers
- Practical correctness engineering
- Database internals for application engineers
- Observability and incident analysis
- Production architecture for AI-assisted teams

Private workshops can be adapted to the runtime, data model, and operational concerns of a specific engineering organization.
