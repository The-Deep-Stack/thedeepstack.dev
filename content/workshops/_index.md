The Deep Stack workshops are designed for engineers who want to reason more clearly about production behaviour—not memorise another collection of architecture patterns.

Sessions combine mechanisms, production examples, guided investigation, and practical exercises. They can be delivered as focused introductions or adapted around an organisation’s technology and operating environment.

## Workshop areas

- Performance engineering for backend engineers
- Practical correctness engineering
- Database internals for application engineers
- Observability and incident analysis
- Production architecture for AI-assisted teams

## Intended audience

Workshops are suitable for application, backend, platform, and infrastructure engineers, as well as technical leads responsible for production systems. The level and examples can be adjusted for a mixed team or a more experienced senior/staff audience.

## Format

A typical engagement includes a preparation conversation, the live workshop, exercises or system walkthroughs, and a concise set of follow-up references. Private workshops can use the team’s runtime, data model, and operational concerns as the working context.
