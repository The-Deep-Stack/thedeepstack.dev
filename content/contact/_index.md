title: Discuss an engineering problem
description: Start a focused conversation about performance, correctness, reliability, architecture, or a team workshop.
---

The most useful first message describes the system, the decision or failure under investigation, its impact, and any relevant time constraint.

Email **[hello@thedeepstack.dev](mailto:hello@thedeepstack.dev)**.

## Useful context to include

- What the system does and which components are involved.
- What has been observed, measured, or ruled out.
- Why the problem matters now.
- The decision, investigation, assessment, or workshop you need.
- Any launch, migration, incident, or planning deadline.

Typical reasons to start a conversation include:

- A service or database is approaching a performance limit.
- A critical distributed workflow has unclear correctness properties.
- A team is preparing a high-risk launch, migration, or scaling event.
- Engineers need a practical workshop grounded in their production environment.
